How it's calculated

Gold value = grams × gold content × gold price per gram.

Break-even = price with tax ÷ (grams × gold content) × 31.1035.

Rise = break-even ÷ today's gold price.

Gold content is karat ÷ 24 (14k = 58.3%). One troy ounce is 31.1035 g. Paying above gold value is normal for jewellery, since the price also covers design, craft and the store's costs.
