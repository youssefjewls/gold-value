This is the value of the gold alone. Finished jewellery sells for more, and gold buyers usually pay 70–90% of it for scrap.

How it's calculated

Gold value = grams × gold content × (gold price per oz ÷ 31.1035).

Break-even gold price = price with tax ÷ (grams × gold content) × 31.1035.

How much gold has to rise = break-even price ÷ today's price. It's the same as price ÷ gold value.

Gold content is karat ÷ 24, so 14k is 58.3%. One troy ounce is 31.1035 g, and one pennyweight (dwt) is 1.5552 g.

Paying more than the gold value is normal for jewellery, because the price also covers design, the work of making it and the store's costs. Bullion coins and bars usually sell for only a few percent above the gold value.
